05 / BEFORE YOU BRIEF US

QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED

How fast can you deliver after an event?

Highlight reels and press cuts are edited on site and can be delivered the same day. Full films with color and sound typically follow within 24 to 72 hours depending on length and formats.

Do you cover government and protocol events?

Yes. Our crews are experienced with official ceremonies, summits and secure venues, and we plan access, positions and dress code with your protocol team in advance.

What formats do you deliver?

Cinema-grade 4K masters, 16:9 web versions, 9:16 and 1:1 social cuts, LED-wall resolutions and Arabic / English variants from a single production.

Where do you operate?

We are based in Dubai and Sharjah and deploy crews to any venue across the UAE at short notice. International productions are handled case by case.

Can you stream an event live?

Yes. Multi-camera live streaming to social platforms, private links or in-venue screens, with graphics and bilingual lower-thirds.

How do we start?

Send a short brief using the form below: the event or campaign, dates, location and what you need delivered. We reply within 24 hours with a crew plan and a quote.
