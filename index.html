07 / BEFORE THE BRIEF

QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED.

Do you work with government and public-sector entities?

Yes. SMV supports government, semi-government and institutional communication teams across the UAE. Our workflow can be adapted to formal approval structures, protocol requirements and confidential assignments.

Can you deliver content on the same day as an event?

Yes, when rapid delivery is scoped in advance. We agree the priority moments, edit formats, review route and release deadlines before the event, then assign an on-site content team accordingly. Timing depends on the number and complexity of deliverables.

Can you work in Arabic and English?

We can plan bilingual productions and deliver Arabic and English versions, including scripts, graphics, subtitles and platform adaptations. The language workflow and final approval authority are confirmed at the start.

Can you coordinate filming and aerial permissions?

We identify location and aerial requirements during pre-production and can coordinate the relevant permissions within the agreed scope. Requirements, lead times and approvals vary by emirate, venue and airspace.

How do you handle confidential projects?

We can work under NDA and follow project-specific rules for access, communications, review, storage and handover. Share the confidentiality requirements at enquiry stage so the correct workflow can be included in the proposal.

What formats can you deliver?

We deliver master films and channel-ready versions for broadcast, websites, press distribution, social platforms, vertical screens and event displays. Resolution, aspect ratios, subtitles, safe areas and file specifications are agreed before production.

Do you cover all seven emirates?

Yes, UAE-wide assignments can be supported subject to production scope, permits, travel and schedule.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

The objective, intended audience, event or launch date, location, required deliverables, review deadline and procurement route are the best starting points. If the brief is still developing, we can help define the production scope.
