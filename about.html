ABOUT SMV

A PRODUCTION PARTNER FOR MOMENTS THAT MATTER.

Singer Media Vision is a UAE-based film and content production company working across official events, institutional communications, campaigns and live experiences. We bring creative judgement and production discipline together, helping clients move from an important brief to a release-ready body of work with confidence.

BUILT IN THE UAE. SHAPED BY ITS PACE AND AMBITION.

SMV has grown around a simple belief: strong visual work should make the client's message clearer and the production process easier. Our teams are assembled for the assignment and led through one accountable point of contact, from the first production conversation to the final handover.

We understand the pace of the UAE's event calendar, the importance of cultural and institutional context, and the need to create assets that perform across official, editorial and social channels.
