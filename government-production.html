GOVERNMENT PRODUCTION

OFFICIAL COMMUNICATIONS DESERVE PRODUCTION CERTAINTY.

SMV supports government communication, protocol and event teams with end-to-end film production designed around clear messages, formal approvals, sensitive schedules and multi-channel delivery.

DISCUSS AN ASSIGNMENT REQUEST CREDENTIALS

BECAUSE THE PROCESS MATTERS AS MUCH AS THE FRAME.

A government production may involve leadership offices, communication teams, protocol, security, venues, partners, broadcasters and multiple approval authorities. Our role is to bring these requirements into one workable production plan, protecting the message while keeping the team ready for the realities of a live assignment.
