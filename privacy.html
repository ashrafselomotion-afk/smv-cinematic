PRIVACY NOTICE

HOW WE HANDLE YOUR INFORMATION.

This notice describes what happens to the information you share through this website.

THE BRIEF FORM.

When you submit the brief form, the details you enter are delivered to SMV by email through the form service FormSubmit. We use this information only to assess and respond to your enquiry. We do not sell it or share it with third parties for marketing.

COOKIES AND ANALYTICS.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Your display-theme preference (light or dark) is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

SENSITIVE MATERIAL.

Do not submit classified, restricted or personally sensitive material through the website. Contact us to agree a suitable exchange method where required.

QUESTIONS.

For any question about this notice or to request deletion of information you have sent us, email mustafasinger@smv.ae.
